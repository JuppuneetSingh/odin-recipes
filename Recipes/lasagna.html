Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tomato sauce
  • Tomato paste
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Egg
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the onion and garlic.
  3. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
  4. Add tomato sauce, tomato paste, salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.
  5. Simmer the meat sauce for several minutes.
  6. Mix the ricotta cheese with the egg and some Parmesan cheese.
  7. Layer the meat sauce, lasagna noodles, and ricotta mixture in a baking dish.
  8. Add mozzarella and Parmesan cheese on top.
  9. Repeat the layers until all the ingredients are used.
  10. Bake the lasagna until the cheese is melted and golden.
  11. Let it cool for a few minutes before serving.
