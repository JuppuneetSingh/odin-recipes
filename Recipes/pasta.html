Ingredients

  • Pasta noodles
  • Tomato sauce
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Basil
  • Olives
  • Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta noodles according to the package instructions.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the garlic and onion.
  3. Add the tomato sauce and simmer for several minutes.
  4. Add the cooked pasta to the sauce and mix well.
  5. Garnish with fresh basil and Parmesan cheese before serving.
